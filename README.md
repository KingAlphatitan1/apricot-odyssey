# Apricot Odyssey

Business admin home for Apricot Odyssey LLC.

## What goes here
- Money ledger and income tracking
- Tax documents and filing checklists (e.g. Form 1065 packet)
- Contracts and business correspondence records
- Job hunt log and application records

## Filing rules
- Financial records use exact figures and dates; never invent amounts
- Tax and legal documents are records only; nothing here is legal or tax advice
- Private only, always.
